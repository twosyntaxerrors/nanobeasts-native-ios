Nanobeasts is a freemium walking app. No account is required to play.

The subscription paywall can be opened from Settings > Nanobeasts Pro. Free users receive up to 2,500 steps of creature progression per day. Premium removes that cap and unlocks additional creature families, weekly creature drops, Apple Health progress, and deeper walking insights.

Apple Health access is optional; the app can use device pedometer tracking without it. Monthly and yearly subscriptions unlock the same premium entitlement through RevenueCat. Restore Purchases is available from the paywall.

Friends leaderboard (optional, free): tap the FRIENDS / RACE readout next to the streak at the top of Home. The leaderboard asks for Sign in with Apple only at that point; any Apple ID works and no name or email is requested. After signing in, the board shows your own weekly and daily step totals, with "Invite someone" slots that share an invite link (nanobeasts.app/i/CODE). A friend opens the link, or enters the code with "Enter invite code", to join. Step totals are visible only to friends you add. Account deletion is in the same screen under Account > Delete account. It deletes the server data and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.
