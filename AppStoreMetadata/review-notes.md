Nanobeasts is a freemium walking app. No account is required.

The subscription paywall can be opened from Settings > Nanobeasts Pro. Free users receive up to 2,500 steps of creature progression per day. Premium removes that cap and unlocks additional creature families, weekly creature drops, Apple Health progress, and deeper walking insights.

Apple Health access is optional; the app can use device pedometer tracking without it. Monthly and yearly subscriptions unlock the same premium entitlement through RevenueCat. Restore Purchases is available from the paywall.
