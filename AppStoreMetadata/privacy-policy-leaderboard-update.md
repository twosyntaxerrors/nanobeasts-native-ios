# Privacy policy changes for friends leaderboards

Paste these into https://nanobeasts.app/privacy. Each item says what to replace or
where to add it. Update "Last updated" to the day you publish.

---

## 1. Summary (replace the whole paragraph)

> Nanobeasts works without an account. If you choose to join the friends
> leaderboard, you sign in with Apple and we store your display name, current
> creature, and daily step totals so the friends you add can see them. Health and
> route records otherwise stay on your devices. Optional Apple services,
> purchases, artwork downloads, and sharing are explained below. We do not sell
> personal data or use health data for advertising.

## 2. About this policy (replace the last sentence)

Replace "You can use the app without creating a Nanobeasts account." with:

> You can use the app without creating a Nanobeasts account. An account is only
> needed for the optional friends leaderboard.

## 3. Apple Health, motion, and workout data (replace the third paragraph)

> Nanobeasts does not upload your Health samples, workout measurements, or GPS
> route archive to a Nanobeasts-operated cloud database. The one exception is the
> optional friends leaderboard: if you sign in to it, your daily step totals are
> uploaded as described in "Friends and leaderboards." We do not sell health data
> or use it for advertising, advertising tracking, or marketing profiles. Apple
> controls any Apple Health synchronization or backup you enable.

## 4. New section: "Friends and leaderboards" (add after "Apple Watch, widgets, and device transfers")

> **Friends and leaderboards**
>
> The leaderboard is optional and off until you choose to join it. To join, you
> sign in with Apple. We receive a stable identifier from Apple for your account.
> We do not request your Apple ID email address or the name on your Apple
> account.
>
> When you're signed in, Nanobeasts sends our server:
>
> - your Nanobeasts player name, used as your display name;
> - which creature you're currently raising, shown as your picture;
> - your time zone, so weeks reset at your local midnight; and
> - one step total per day for the current and previous week. Steps you typed
>   into Apple Health by hand are left out.
>
> We don't upload individual Health samples, workouts, routes, locations, or any
> other Health data.
>
> Your display name, creature, and daily and weekly step totals are visible only
> to friends you add, and theirs are visible to you. You add friends by sharing
> your invite link or code. Anyone who has your link or code can add you, so
> share it only with people you want on your leaderboard. You can remove a friend
> at any time, and they will no longer see your steps.
>
> Leaderboard data is stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider, on servers in
> the United States. Your sign-in token is stored in your device's Keychain.

## 5. Retention and deletion (add this paragraph at the start)

> You can delete your leaderboard account at any time from the leaderboard
> screen (Account, then Delete account). This permanently deletes your profile,
> friend connections, and uploaded step totals from our server, and revokes the
> app's Sign in with Apple access. Signing out keeps your account but stops
> uploads from that device. Deleting the app does not delete your leaderboard
> account; delete it in the app first, or email us.

## 6. Children (no change needed)

The existing children section already covers this.
