# Nanobeasts core loop demo

A concise field-journal sequence for the onboarding “Here’s how it works” page. The viewer should understand the product without seeing a walking dashboard: real steps create energy, energy hatches a companion, the companion becomes a Field Dex entry, and consistency earns badges.

Format: 1080 × 1666 portrait, 15.8 seconds, 30 fps, silent and loopable.

Brand truth: background #09090b, surface #171b1a, teal #59e5d3, cyan #19cbe5, achievement gold #f6c95d, white #f6faf9, secondary #a1a1aa. SF Pro Display Bold for outcome captions, Aldrich for branded labels, Space Mono Bold for metrics. No em dashes.

The art uses the real Tutoria egg, Tutoria creature, Field Dex language, and streak badge from the Nanobeasts product. The composition is a visual explanation, not a product ad: no narration, no fake outcomes, no weight-loss promise, and no extra Professor Nano character.
