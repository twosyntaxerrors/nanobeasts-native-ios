# Batch 8 Prompt Set

All concepts use the approved 10K Steps, 5 Day Streak, and First Hatch badges
as finish/quality references. Shared direction: colorful dimensional enamel,
Nanobeasts field-research instruments, strong app-scale silhouettes, white
review backgrounds, and no generic medal/esports treatment.

1. **Dex Cartographer / 35 species**  
   An unfolding biome-atlas sphere with forest, aquatic, crystal, and volcanic
   enamel panels surrounding a luminous specimen-map core.

2. **Gold Collector / 50 species**  
   A golden biodiversity reactor with five colorful biological specimen gems
   orbiting a faceted gene prism.

3. **90 Day Streak**  
   The exact `90` built from a crimson phoenix-flame circuit and an amber solar
   plasma ring joined by a cyan research pulse.

4. **120 Day Streak**  
   The exact `120` built as a connected cyan ignition rod, violet plasma
   conduit, and amber fusion chamber.

5. **Trekker / distance tier 75**  
   Three suspended habitat beacons linked by a luminous climbing research
   cableway toward an unknown signal.

6. **Journey / distance tier 100**  
   A miniature multi-biome world wrapped by a grand waypoint-lit expedition
   orbit ending at a transmitting field station.
