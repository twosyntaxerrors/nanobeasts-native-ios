# Batch 12 Badge Redesigns

## 1. Goal Getter

A cyan path pierces a closed biological target bud, causing coral-and-mint
petals to snap open around a golden check-shaped seed. This replaces the
rejected generic flag treatment.

## 2. Three Evolutions

Three differently colored chrysalis shells curve around a luminous spiral at
intact, cracking, and fully opened stages. Their composition traces a custom
`3` while three golden cores mark the completed evolutions.

## 3. 14 Day Streak

A custom `14` built from an electric-cyan flame lance and interlocking
coral-violet flame ribbons. Fourteen amber continuity nodes run through the
numeral, connected by one traveling ember.

## 4. 21 Day Streak

The approved linked-flame concept is preserved while its upper yellow and lower
red flames are reshaped into a clear custom `21`. The middle orange flame and
teal electrical seams remain the continuity system.

## 5. Sprint Master

An unmistakable runner launches from a fractured coral starting surface in the
first explosive stride. A golden impact burst and compressed violet speed arcs
make the takeoff motion readable.

## 6. Goal Veteran

An evolved indigo goal-core displays five biological armor fans. Each fan
carries ten colored completion lights around a mint check crystal, representing
fifty goal completions without a generic printed `50`.
