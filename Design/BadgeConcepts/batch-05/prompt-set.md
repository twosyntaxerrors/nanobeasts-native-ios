# Batch 5 Prompt Set

All six concepts used the approved 10K Steps, 5 Day Streak, and First Hatch
assets as style/quality references. Shared direction: premium front-facing
dimensional hard-enamel Nanobeasts field-research badges, polished dark metal
rims, vivid jewel enamel, crisp geometry, strong readability at 72–78 pt,
centered on white, no words/letters/numerals, and no generic esports motifs.

1. **Nest Keeper / 15 hatches**  
   A protective high-tech hatchery nest holding several visually distinct
   colorful eggs, two freshly cracked shell halves, and a warm incubation glow.
   The abundance of successful hatching should read without counting objects.

2. **21 Day Streak**  
   Three interlocking circular energy loops, each partly shaped like a flame,
   linked into one uninterrupted ascending path. Small day lights support the
   continuity metaphor without using a calendar or grid.

3. **10 Species**  
   An open futuristic specimen archive with six large colorful biological
   windows—leaf, wing, fin, flame, antenna, and crystalline motifs—surrounding
   a central scanning lens.

4. **30 Goal Days**  
   A fully closed orbit of illuminated day nodes surrounding a central goal
   beacon and check-shaped light path. No missing or dim nodes.

5. **Distance Tier 10**  
   A unit-neutral expedition map: a luminous winding route leaves a field lab,
   crosses a ridge and river, passes two waypoints, and reaches a distant signal
   beacon.

6. **Sprint Master**  
   A sleek cyan-and-coral field boot transformed into a kinetic meteor, with
   three speed fins, a bright trailing arc, and a finish beacon bursting behind
   it.
