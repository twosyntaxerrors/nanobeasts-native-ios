# Batch 10 Badge Concepts

All concepts were generated as centered, square badge artwork on a clean white
review background. They avoid generic medal, shield, ribbon, varsity-type, and
heavy metallic-frame treatments.

## 1. 120 Day Streak

An organic seasonal emblem built directly into a custom `120`: a mint leaf
forms the `1`, a violet living loop forms the `2`, and an amber seed-ring forms
the `0`. Small spring, summer, autumn, and winter accents communicate sustained
progress without a surrounding metal plate.

## 2. 180 Day Streak

A custom `180` made from an icy cyan energy stroke, a magenta-violet infinity
loop, and a golden solar-flame ring. The starry teal backing stays soft and
self-contained instead of reading as a manufactured metal badge frame.

## 3. Voyager

A wide four-biome expedition panorama. A luminous current travels through
coral sunrise, forest, ice-ocean, and a violet night mountain before reaching a
guiding star. The horizontal journey reads as long-range exploration without a
hat, crescent, compass, or text.

## 4. Wanderer

A wind-carried seed follows a teal current while its drifting fragments become
coral, leaf, water, and crystal forms. The badge represents moving freely
through multiple habitats without using a path sign or footprint.

## 5. Wayfarer

An emerald leaf monolith and violet crystal monolith form an open natural
gateway. A cyan-and-gold route passes between them toward a distant star, with
water and ember elements marking the beginning of the journey.

## 6. Trailblazer

A coral-orange ignition wedge breaks through unexplored indigo crystalline
terrain and creates a new cyan route. Leaf, water, and crystal sparks follow
through the opening, making the badge read as creating a path where none
existed.
