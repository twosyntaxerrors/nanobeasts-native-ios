# Batch 7 Prompt Set

All concepts use the approved 10K Steps, 5 Day Streak, and First Hatch badges
as finish/quality references. Shared direction: colorful dimensional enamel,
Nanobeasts field-research instruments, strong app-scale silhouettes, white
review backgrounds, and no generic medal/esports treatment.

1. **Field Archivist / 20 species**  
   A mechanical archive tower opening into four biological data drawers around
   a luminous central specimen core.

2. **Silver Collector / 25 species**  
   A bright silver Möbius archive ring wrapping a cyan prism, with five colorful
   specimen capsules embedded along its endless loop.

3. **45 Day Streak**  
   The exact `45` engineered from a continuous amber/coral flame circuit with a
   cyan pulse traveling through the entire path.

4. **60 Day Streak**  
   The exact `60` embodied as a red-orange comet feeding an unbroken golden
   solar ring with cyan orbital pulse nodes.

5. **Explorer / distance tier 25**  
   A three-lobed field scanner containing forest, river, and crystal-canyon
   habitats connected by a single amber route to an unknown signal.

6. **Expedition / distance tier 50**  
   A rugged portable field laboratory with an egg-safe chamber, scanner,
   antenna, sample vials, and a long route wrapping toward a cyan signal star.
