# Batch 12 revision prompt set

All concepts were rendered on a clean white review background as polished enamel game badges. They avoid generic varsity typography and will only be converted to transparent production assets after approval.

1. **Goal Getter** — A circular field target with concentric cyan telemetry rings, a coral route arrow striking a gold bullseye, and a mint completion check. The target is the unmistakable subject; no fruit, plant, or trophy silhouette.
2. **Three Evolutions** — A connected three-stage research badge built from the actual Ampaw → Ampunch → Ampact family. It carries the family's yellow body color, blue forehead arrow, blue ear discs, green eyes, electric tail capacitor, and progressively larger blue gauntlets.
3. **14 Day Streak** — A readable integrated `14` constructed from the actual Flarva/Igneatoad visual language: coral and orange flame forms, cream spirals, charcoal bands, and glowing ember nodes.
4. **21 Day Streak** — A readable integrated `21` constructed from Lavacrook's black volcanic armor, molten orange seams, golden flame/horn forms, and magma nodes.
5. **Sprint Master** — Actual Ampaw launching through a curved Nanobeasts research track, with its yellow/blue palette, electric capacitor tail, and a compact checkered finish beacon.
6. **Goal Veteran** — A pentagonal field shield centered on a gold bullseye and mint completion check, with nested cyan goal chevrons, five completion light bars, and a subtle finish-line grid.
