# Batch 9 Prompt Set

All concepts use the approved 10K Steps, 5 Day Streak, First Hatch, and 45 Day
Streak badges as finish/quality references. Shared direction: colorful
dimensional enamel, Nanobeasts field-research instruments, strong app-scale
silhouettes, white review backgrounds, and no generic medal/esports treatment.

1. **90 Day Streak — revision**  
   A compact `90` engineered from linked ember and amber kinetic-energy coils,
   crossed by a precise cyan research circuit.

2. **120 Day Streak — revision**  
   An eclipse chronometer whose cyan meridian needle, violet orbit, and amber
   eclipse ring form the exact `120`, surrounded by four seasonal nodes.

3. **Journey / distance tier 100 — revision**  
   The recognizable Earth wrapped by one continuous black-metal and cyan-energy
   research ring with three waypoint lights and a field scanner.

4. **180 Day Streak**  
   A deep-space chronometer whose meridian needle, twin orbit loops, and eclipse
   ring form `180`, joined by a single teal pulse.

5. **Adventurer / distance tier 150**  
   Three receding jungle, ice, and desert habitat gateways connected by one
   luminous research trail leading to an unknown signal.

6. **Voyager / distance tier 200**  
   A crescent deep-field route instrument carrying five biome relays from a
   coral sunrise toward a brilliant cyan discovery signal.
