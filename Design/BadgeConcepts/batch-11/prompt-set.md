# Batch 11 Badge Concepts

This batch completes the first custom-design pass across the entire 64-badge
catalog. Each concept is centered on a white review background and avoids
generic medal, shield, ribbon, footprint, and heavy metallic-frame treatments.

## 1. 3 Day Streak

Three linked living-flame forms create a custom `3`. Coral, amber, and violet
segments are joined by cyan continuity nodes and wrapped by a mint energy trail
to communicate three consecutive days.

## 2. Triple Threat

Three colorful goal-energy creatures orbit a dark triangular success core. Each
direction includes a completed target mark, and their combined motion creates a
central three-point burst.

## 3. Ranger

A vigilant leaf-eye scans three living habitat layers—forest, water, and coral
stone. The asymmetric monitoring composition communicates a field ranger
watching over multiple ecosystems.

## 4. Pathfinder

An irregular living maze contains many dark dead ends while one cyan route
threads through coral, leaf, and crystal passages and becomes gold at a
star-shaped destination.

## 5. Pilgrim

One luminous seed-core changes through coral ember, mint sprout, aqua winged
seed, and violet celestial bloom. A single gold thread connects every stage of
the long transformative journey.

## 6. The Proclaimer

A legendary coral-and-gold signal bloom sends two enormous journey waves
through water, sky, crystal, and leaf motifs. The waves travel outward and meet
again at a radiant star, announcing completion of the catalog's final distance
milestone.
