# Localization market research

Status: research only. The owner explicitly requested that localization not proceed yet. No translations, binary changes, App Store metadata, screenshots, territorial availability, subscription availability, or uploads have been changed. The owner is considering English and Japanese first, then approximately five priority markets.

## Evidence

- Pokémon GO: Sensor Tower's August 2026 analysis covers cumulative July 2016–July 2026 performance. Its revenue chart ranks the United States, Japan, Germany, United Kingdom, and Canada in that order. The US and Japan account for more than 65% of estimated store revenue. The download chart instead ranks the US, Brazil, India, Mexico, and Indonesia; download volume is not the same as spending. These estimates combine App Store and Google Play and exclude web purchases; they are not an iOS-only subscription-market ranking. [Sensor Tower report](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/pokemon-go-10th-anniversary-JP). The linked chart was visually inspected.
- Pikmin Bloom: PocketGamer.biz's July 1, 2026 report, citing AppMagic, assigns Taiwan 52% and Japan 23% of H1 2026 spending across the two main app stores. Japan still leads lifetime spending at 49%. South Korea is described as the fourth most lucrative market; the sentence does not explicitly identify its measurement period. This report does not provide a complete five-country ranking. [Report](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/pikmin-bloom-skyrockets-to-record-revenue-as-taiwanese-spending-surges-902/).

These are market-selection signals from established intellectual properties and different monetization models, not forecasts of Nanobeasts conversion or revenue.

## Recommended shortlist, pending owner's decision

For five priority markets: United States (English), Japan (Japanese), Taiwan (Traditional Chinese localized for Taiwan), Germany (German), and South Korea (Korean). Suggested language sequence is English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, German, Korean. This is a recommendation combining the two competitors, not a claimed shared top-five ranking. English also serves English-speaking users in the UK, Canada, and Australia without a new language translation; regional store metadata may be tailored separately. If the owner wants to minimize first-release work, English plus Japanese is a coherent initial scope, with Taiwan the next translation opportunity.

## Availability and language are separate

An English-only app can be downloaded in other territories when app availability is enabled there. Localizing the App Store listing improves how it is presented and searched; it does not translate the app binary. App language localization covers the iPhone and Watch experience, onboarding, permissions, purchase/trial language, notifications, dates, numbers, and units. When implementation is authorized, prioritize a consistent translated product and store page, with human review of Japanese and paid subscription copy.

For a hard paywall, intended markets must have both the app and the subscriptions available. No availability state was inspected or changed in this research turn.
