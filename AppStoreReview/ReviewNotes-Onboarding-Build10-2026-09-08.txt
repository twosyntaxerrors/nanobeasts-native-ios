Nanobeasts turns walking into a creature-collection journey. No email, account, or sign-in is required. The name field is optional and comes after the user's walking goals and barriers.

An active Nanobeasts Pro subscription or trial is required to enter the main app. US plans are $4.99/month with no trial, or $29.99/year with a seven-day free trial for eligible yearly subscribers. Localized prices and eligibility come from the store. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall and the returning-subscriber screen. Monthly and yearly plans grant the same Premium entitlement. The release build uses nanobeasts_native_premium_monthly and nanobeasts_native_premium_yearly.

Users may close pricing to return to their saved evolution-plan recap, two steps before checkout. This does not unlock the app. Onboarding answers are saved locally, so termination does not restart the questionnaire. When a subscription expires, the user's progress and workout history remain saved, and a renewal screen provides restoration, subscription management, notification preferences, privacy, terms, and support.

Notifications are optional. The onboarding permission explanation expressly includes up to five setup reminders, two days apart, for users who leave before subscribing. These use local notifications, without a remote push service or name upload. They stop after the fifth message, purchase/trial activation, onboarding completion, or opt-out. Users can opt out during onboarding, on the paywall, or in Settings. No discount or trial eligibility is promised in reminder copy.

Onboarding step charts show an illustrative path from an estimated baseline to a suggested, editable goal; they are not measured or guaranteed results.

After onboarding, grant optional Apple Health access for supported background activity. The workout button opens the iPhone workout hub, with a first-use tour of the map, activity picker, goals, start control, and History. The tour can be skipped and reopened using the question-mark button. It does not interrupt an active/resumed workout. With location enabled, outdoor workouts can record a route and permanently clear explored map areas. Completed workouts with routes offer replay and video sharing with a creature/egg marker and stats.

The Apple Watch companion receives the iPhone's subscription access state, current creature/egg, step goal, and accent. Start or restore a subscription on iPhone and open both apps to synchronize. An in-progress workout remains available to finish/save if access expires; new workouts require active access. Please use physical devices and actual movement to evaluate HealthKit, GPS, and paired connectivity; missing Health samples are not fabricated.

The native bundle is com.twosyntaxerrors.nanobeasts.native. The separate, unreleased Expo app record is retained independently.
